FAQ

I lost my history switching iPhones

Data lives on the device and is included in the iCloud backup. Restoring that backup on the new device brings your history back.

I cancelled. Do I lose my days?

No. The counter, history and logs stay yours and keep working. What locks is the full program (phases, predictive alerts and clinical report).

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sem Vape → Cancel subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the subscription screen, tap "Restore purchases" using the same Apple ID as the original purchase.

I set the wrong quit date

Settings → Program → edit the date. The app recalculates everything.

I vaped. What now?

Log it: Settings → Register relapse. Nothing in your history is erased — the counter restarts with context and the plan adjusts.
