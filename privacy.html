Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

In short: Sem Vape does not collect your data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. No account, no server of ours, no tracking.

What the app stores

Stored on your device only: your first name, how much you vaped and the pod price, your quit date, reasons, triggers, daily check-ins, craving logs and savings goals.

Where it goes

Nowhere. We run no servers and keep no user database, and we cannot see anything you write. Data lives in the app’s local storage and is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), under your Apple account’s control — not ours.

We do not collect

  • Email, phone number, address or any personal identifier
  • Location
  • Device contacts (the support contact is typed by you and stays local)
  • Usage analytics or advertising identifiers

Notifications

Notifications are generated and scheduled on the device. No server pushes anything to you, and permission can be revoked anytime in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never receive or store payment data — only Apple’s confirmation that an active entitlement exists.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete my account removes everything from the device immediately. Since nothing ever left it, there is nothing to request elsewhere.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 17.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it and the new version applies from publication.
