What the app is

Sem Vape is a behavioral support app for people quitting vaping: a nicotine-free counter, a phased program, trigger and craving logging, and tools for the hard moments.

What it is not: it is not medical treatment, does not replace a healthcare professional and makes no diagnosis. Recovery estimates come from public health literature (WHO) and serve as general reference — not as an assessment of your case. See a doctor for clinical guidance, especially if you use cessation medication.

Subscriptions

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Manage and cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused portion is forfeited when you subscribe.
  • The lifetime purchase is one-time, with no renewal.
